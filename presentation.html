Engineering Faculty Demo Day

FP3 —
Finding Project Partners.

A tag-based matching platform that connects students with the right professor for their next paper, thesis, or research project.

A gap between two groups.

02 · Problem

Discovery is verbal

Students only meet professors they already know whom to email.

Professors recruit blind

The student body's interests are invisible past a tiny social radius.

Faculty research is scattered

Across DBLP, Scholar, faculty pages — none of it surfaces to undergrads.

One shared vocabulary of tags.

03 · Solution

Both sides describe their interests with the same tag set. The platform ranks counterparts by overlap.

1

Identify

Pick tags from a curated, category-grouped pool. AI assists by extracting tags from your bio & publications.

2

Match

Weighted overlap surfaces counterparts in a personalized "Recommended for you" feed — both directions.

3

Act

Apply, accept, reject, save. Lightweight flow turns discovery into a real research collaboration.

08 · ML

Users write free text — abstracts, bios, project descriptions. We need to project that messy text onto a fixed, curated tag vocabulary so matching is consistent. Self-reported tags are too noisy; AI does the projection.

▸ Why cosine similarity?
sim(A, B) = cos(θ) = A · B / (‖A‖ · ‖B‖)

Small angle ⇒ similar meaning. Direction matters, length doesn't — robust to text length differences.
