Problem Definition

  • Students cannot easily find professors whose research areas match their interests.
  • Professors need a structured way to discover students and academic partners.
  • Projects, publication ideas and team opportunities are scattered across informal channels.
  • There is no central skill/tag-based discovery and collaboration workflow.
Manual searchLow visibilityMissed collaboration

Project Objectives

  • Build a university-only platform using @ostimteknik.edu.tr authentication.
  • Match students with professors for projects and publications.
  • Recommend open projects based on student profile tags and interests.
  • Enable professor-led teams such as 2 professors + 10 students.
